# Yuki Illustration Approval Gate

Illustrations are part of Yuki’s product behavior and identity, not decorative filler. Core visual work must stop for user approval before generation, replacement, or material restyling.

## Approval brief

The request for approval must identify:

- the visual job and the world space or screen it supports;
- scene composition and responsive crop behavior;
- spatial perspective and camera relationship;
- Yuki’s silhouette, form, palette, expression range, and idle behaviors;
- environment objects, density, and interaction affordances;
- watercolor, paper grain, pencil, linen, wood, lighting, and shadow treatment;
- default atmosphere and low-stimulation variant;
- visual responses to tap, hold, drag, approach, drawing, staying, and silence;
- whether the asset is a full scene, layered composition, reusable object, or hybrid.

## Approval sequence

1. Describe the proposed direction in user-facing language.
2. Wait for explicit approval or requested changes.
3. Create the smallest useful visual proof or approved asset set.
4. Show an intermediate visual checkpoint.
5. Ask whether the direction is ready to expand and lock into the shared visual system.
6. Integrate only the approved result.

## Reuse and accessibility

Approved visual anchors should be reused across web and mobile to prevent style drift and reduce asset weight. Every meaningful visual state needs a non-color cue and a sound-off path. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and Yuki’s low-stimulation controls. Illustrations must not imply diagnoses, emotional certainty, or surveillance.
